Calendar and notifications

Every deadline, session and forum close date in one place.

Academic dates live in six or seven places at most institutions. Here they arrive in one calendar, typed by what they are, and a faculty member can switch off the types they are not thinking about.

  • Three views — Month, Week and Schedule, with Schedule listing what is upcoming first.
  • Relative dating — entries read as in 15 days or 9 days ago, so nothing has to be worked out against today.
  • Course-scoped notifications — every notification carries the course it came from, so a reply is never context-free.

Outcomes and attainment

Map the outcome while you build the course, not the week before the audit.

Outcome attainment is usually reconstructed after the fact, which is why it takes weeks. Course and programme outcomes are defined once, attached to the assignments and questions that measure them, and calculated from work students have already submitted.

2

3

4

Report on it

Pull outcome reports on demand, ready for the submissions KenCompliance handles.
